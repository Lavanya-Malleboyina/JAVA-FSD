Tags:
    ->Tags are pre-defined words which are enclosed with angular brackets
Ex: <h1> , <meta> , <title> , <p> , <figure> , <li> ..etc 

Types:

1. Paired tags
    ->Any tags having both opening and closing tags

    Ex:

i. All heading tags

    Ex:

    <h1>  </h1>
    <h2>  </h2>
    <h3>  </h3>
    <h4>  </h4>
    <h5>  </h5>
    <h6>  </h6>

ii. Formatting tags :
Ex:

<i> </i>            ->italic <em></em>           ->italic <b></b>             ->bold <strong></strong>   ->bold <u></u>             ->underline <strike></strike>   ->to strike text <sub></sub>         ->SubScript <sup></sup>         ->Super Script <mark></mark>       ->to highlight text

<center></center>   ->content at center 
<marquee></marquee> ->to scroll content in different directions

2. Unpaired tags / Self closing tags
    ->Any Tags having only opening tags

Ex: <br> , <hr>, <img> , <col>, <input> , <meta> ..etc

Attribute :
    ->Attributes are the additional information given to the tags
    ->Attributes are always written inside the opening tag

Ex:

<h1 align="center" id="heading"> </h1> 

or

<img src="" alt="" height="" width="">

HTML Elements :

->Opening tag, closing tag and the content together is called an HTML Element

Ex:

<h1> Heading </h1>

<p> Paragraph </p>

Form tags :

    <form></form>
    <fieldset></fieldset>
    <legend></legend>     
    <label> </label>    
    <input> 
    <textarea> </textarea>  
    <button></button>  
    <select></select>
