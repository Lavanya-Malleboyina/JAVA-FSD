animations: to animate CSS elements
@keyframe myName{
    from{font-size:20px;}
    to{font-size:40px;}
}
Animation Properties
    1.animation-name 
    2.animation-duration
    3.animation-timing-function
    4.animation-delay
    5.animation-iteration-count
    6.animation-direction
Animation Shorthand
    animation: rotate 2s linear 2s infinite normal;
